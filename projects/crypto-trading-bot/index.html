How it works

Strategy

  • RSI (Relative Strength Index) measures how strongly the price has been rising or falling over the last 14 candles, on a 0–100 scale, using Wilder’s smoothing.
  • Buy when RSI falls below 30 (oversold), spending 25% of the cash.
  • Sell the whole position when RSI rises above 70 (overbought).
  • Signals are only taken when a candle closes, so a signal can’t vanish after the bot has acted on it. A 0.1% fee is charged on every trade, like Binance spot trading.

Code structure

  • BinanceMarket loads recent candles over REST, then streams live candles over a WebSocket, reconnecting with back-off if the connection drops.
  • SimulatedMarket has the same interface and takes over if Binance can’t be reached.
  • RsiStrategy, PaperPortfolio and TradingBot hold the trading logic, separate from the page, and are unit-tested.
  • MarketChart draws the price and RSI on a canvas, with a hover crosshair.
